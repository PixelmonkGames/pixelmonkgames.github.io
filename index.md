# PixelMonk Games

PixelMonk Games is an independent mobile game developer creating simple and enjoyable games for Android.

## Kitty Match: Cute Cat Catcher

Kitty Match is a portrait arcade game where players match falling cats with the correct coloured catcher, earn points, and compete for high scores.

Google Play Games sign-in is used to identify the player and provide access to the Kitty Match leaderboard. Players can compare their scores with other players through Google Play Games.

[Download Kitty Match on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.PixelMonk.KittyMatch)
